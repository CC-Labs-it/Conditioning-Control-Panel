SECOND LAP: 7.2.2

FIXES
- Racing Thoughts: Boudoir no longer gets stuck at "Preparing track". the course builds about six times faster, and a slow PC gets time to finish loading instead of the window closing on it.
- the launcher redraws any tile that stays invisible after it resizes or after you come back from a game.
- Dynamic wallpaper keeps pictures in shape on vertical monitors: it fills the screen and trims the sides instead of squashing them.
- Circe's Tab: the raffle card says its total is net. time anyone takes off during the month comes out of it.
- strict lock cards say what really happens: Escape is the default panic key, and panic always ends a card. want Escape to do nothing on a card? bind panic to another key in Settings.

NEW
- Pink Filter: a new switch, "Under spirals and text", puts the tint under spirals, subliminals, bubbles and text so they keep their own colours. off by default.

HUNTING A STRAY SOUND
some of you hear short music stings that come from nowhere. bug reports now also list sounds played by web pages and the bubble video. if you hear one, send an in-app bug report right away and it should name the source.
