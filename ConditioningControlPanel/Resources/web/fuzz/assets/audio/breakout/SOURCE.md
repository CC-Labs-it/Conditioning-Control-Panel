# Mini Breakout audio

These are renders of the Backroom Breakout station's own Web Audio instruments in
`Resources/web/backroom/stations/breakout/audio.js` and `cues/power.js`.
The music is the steady-state four-bar colour loop at 96 BPM and 0.84 saturation.
The four contact recordings use the station's `hit` function. The capsule bell
and both catch motifs use `powerDrop` and `powerCatch`. The shield save uses the
perfect-return bell. The clear cue uses `wallCleared`.

All clips are 24 kHz mono PCM. The ten-second bed loops at the sample boundary.
The in-game mixer supplies stereo pan, source levels, and master volume.
