## Exhibition artwork

The aquarium tunnel, its fish, rays, kelp and caustic patterns were modeled and drawn for this exhibition. The seabed uses Terrazzo Tiles and Marble 01. The kelp uses Oak Veneer 01, the steel scale finish uses Metal009, the colored fins use Blue Metal Plate, and the ray skin uses White Plaster 02. The tank liners use SurfaceImperfections001. The source artists and CC0 material links are listed above.

The conservatory's shade trees and climbing leaf clusters use [Pachira Aquatica 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/pachira_aquatica_01). Its planted beds and growing trays use [Calathea Orbifolia 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/calathea_orbifolia_01) and [Fern 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/fern_02). Rico Cilliers and Rob Tuytel created the three plant collections for Poly Haven. The Expo uses reduced three-dimensional meshes and their original surface coordinates. [Poly Haven CC0 licence](https://polyhaven.com/license)

Rob Tuytel created the conservatory's [Brown Mud Leaves 01](https://polyhaven.com/a/brown_mud_leaves_01) soil and [Bark Brown 02](https://polyhaven.com/a/bark_brown_02) climbing-stem surface. The planted furniture, trellises, gravel and planting layouts were made for the Expo. [Poly Haven CC0 licence](https://polyhaven.com/license)

The green-utopia pavilion uses the CC0 finishes in the table above. The alloy finish uses Metal009. Graphite and lagoon enamel use Blue Metal Plate. The ceramic shell and gallery walls use White Plaster 02. Honey timber uses Oak Veneer 01, honed paving uses Terrazzo Tiles, acoustic flooring uses Poly Wool Herringbone, and edge diffusers use SurfaceImperfections001.

The persimmon control caps and contrasting raised marks use the same CC0 Blue Metal Plate. The reading-room oak, acoustic felt, glazed mineral tiles, zinc service finishes and sea tiles use the credited wood, cloth, plaster, metal and terrazzo maps. The staggered tile joints were drawn for this museum.

The irrigation skid and power benches use silver and copper finishes derived from Lennart Demes's Metal009. The water surface uses his SurfaceImperfections001. The aqua pump and valve finish uses Blue Metal Plate by Rob Tuytel. The gasket texture uses Rough Linen by colormass and Rico Cilliers. The source sets are CC0. The Expo workshop modeled the equipment and drew the instrument faces. [Metal009](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=Metal009) · [SurfaceImperfections001](https://ambientcg.com/view?id=SurfaceImperfections001) · [Blue Metal Plate](https://polyhaven.com/a/blue_metal_plate) · [Rough Linen](https://polyhaven.com/a/rough_linen)

The llama castings, monument, clothed attendant, hoof glove, plush and living-wall cassette were reconstructed with Tripo from project references and finished in Blender. The gallery portraits use the original approved game artwork. Monocles, chains, necklace beads, badges, headwear, clothing details, plinths and plaques were modeled or typeset for this exhibition.

The chronology and statue projectors carry the approved Llama Expo photographs. The west mezzanine has seven separately framed photographs from the project's “Famous photographs, corrected by the Archive” series. Their captions follow the original slide register. The recordings in `narration/hall` are the approved museum-speaker versions. Their exact subtitles and slide associations are in `narration/manifest.json`.

The collection cases use oiled oak from Jenelle van Heerden’s Oak Veneer 01, yarn from Poly Wool Herringbone by colormass and Rico Cilliers, and brushed metal and optical imperfections from Lennart Demes. The museum workshop prepared the new weave, lens reeds, calibration scales and object labels. The archive chess study uses eight original Piece by Piece game pieces. The Technical Forum displays the original Terms & Conditioning classifieds for A lamp with opinions and Discount Stairs, alongside a brass desk lamp and a portable model stair built for this exhibit.
