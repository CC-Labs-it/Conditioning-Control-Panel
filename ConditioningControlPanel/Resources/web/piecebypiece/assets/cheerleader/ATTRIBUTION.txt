Robot Emi and her face atlas are reused from the existing Racing Thoughts 3D assets.

emi-racing.glb derives from the approved seated R4 emi-teacup-kart.glb:
SHA256 7362bfc8d07d36b079e0d086fda15378ca3d5d269bcd3b4fad64df8fb65776f1.
Only triangles belonging to cup_body and saucer_body are removed. Emi's retained
geometry, UVs, palette, skin weights, 15-joint rig, inverse bind matrices, node
transforms, and all 26 authored animations are preserved. Textures are the source
PNG images corresponding to the packed model. No new limbs or body geometry.

emi-racing-faces.png is the approved revision 5 master atlas: sixteen cells in a
4 by 4 grid, using the existing classic Emi expressions. Its packed Racing
Thoughts counterpart has SHA256
591d9d8dfcf74b95e23035961e0781bdaae8dcabc717fdd4c299c68866cb7b08.

Extraction is reproducible with handoff-1117/chess-boards/extract-emi-racing.mjs.
Existing project asset terms apply. No additional third-party assets are introduced.
