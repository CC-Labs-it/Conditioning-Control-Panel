# The exhibition wings

The research wing uses glazed partitions, timber work surfaces and overhead lighting tracks. In the rotunda a suspended optical ring and timber acoustic fins mark the circular room without crossing the portrait projection paths. The warmer brass accessories on the herd belong to the permanent collection.

The Hoof Simulator lends the visitor two cloven gloves, counts missed presses, offers a picture test, and brings in the human attendant. The Habitat shows the run's manual presses and recorded terms in the human diorama. The glass, feeding slot and clause plaque respond separately. One museum narrator speaks in both exhibits.

The sunken Hall of the Twelve reads the player's herd. Each found animal has its own bronze casting, separate fittings, framed portrait, plinth plaque and playable log. Unfound animals have a packing cover, scaffold, hard hat and redacted plaque. Henry has a sealed record, Kevin has a timed silence, and the doctor's alpaca button returns the visitor to the entrance. A complete herd opens the photograph release. The player can carry that photograph into the recap.

Each found statue has its own log grille, replay button and slide carousel beside a pull-down screen. The lamps project the archive photographs assigned to the approved narration. Multi-image logs change slides during the recording. The panel's three llamas stand on the floor behind their old table.

In the shop, the visitor can buy their framed contract for one Yes. The door loops back to the shop. The shelves move, the till offers the exit, and the purchased wormhole returns the player to the game's ending recap.
