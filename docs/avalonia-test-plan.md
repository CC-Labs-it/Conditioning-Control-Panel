# Avalonia tester steps

1. Open Play, then Settings > Devices. Start webcam tracking and return to Play. The chip reads Tracking; Gaze and Focus Gaze are fully opaque and their needs-tracker notices disappear. Lose the face: the chip reads Face lost while both cards remain bright. Stop tracking: both cards dim to 0.62 and show their notices. Tier lockbands remain independent. Change language and revisit Play; the chip uses the selected language. WPF `ConditioningControlPanel/MainWindow/MainWindow.LabTab.cs` (`UpdateLabTrackerUi`, `UpdateWebcamStatusChips`, `WebcamStateText`). Rows: `views-tab-play`
2. Enter Play with motion enabled: the first eight visible wall sections fade from 0 to 1 over 220 ms and rise 10 px over 260 ms, staggered 40 ms (delay capped at six slots). Leave during entrance and return; no old animation survives. With motion Reduced or Off, sections appear immediately (WPF `AnimateTabIn` returns at Reduced before the stagger). WPF `ConditioningControlPanel/MainWindow/MainWindow.ChromeFx.cs` (`AnimateTabIn`, `FindStaggerTargets`, `StaggerTabCards`) and `ConditioningControlPanel/Services/MotionFx.cs` (`StaggerIn`). Rows: `views-tab-play`
