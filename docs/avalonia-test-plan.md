# Avalonia tester steps

1. Enable EmiDesk Offers in App Settings, summon her, open the ring and pick its first unpinned available card. When the authored pin offer is drawn (subject to odds/cooldowns), reopen the ring while the chips wait, then accept it. The reopened ring stays open, the target takes the first pinned slot, and the same target is no longer offered. Unknown or unavailable targets must not produce a chip. Glass remains unported on this head. WPF `ConditioningControlPanel/Services/EmiDesk/EmiOffers.cs:535`. Rows: `views-settings-emidesk`
