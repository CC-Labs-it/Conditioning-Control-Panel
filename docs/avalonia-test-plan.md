# Avalonia tester steps

1. WPF `ConditioningControlPanel/Windows/WebcamQuickRecalWindow.xaml.cs`, `ConditioningControlPanel/MainWindow/MainWindow.xaml.cs` — Rows: `win-webcam-quick-recal`
   On Windows and X11, start a calibrated, consented tracker; press Ctrl+Alt+G with the shell visible, minimized, and hidden. Confirm one dot on the calibrated monitor, 600 ms ready, 2 s sampling, success text and 350 ms hold; the tracker remains running. Cancel with Escape and panic; prior offset is restored. With tracking stopped, the shortcut starts it temporarily and stops it after closing. With consent or calibration missing, it silently refuses. A second press must not stack a dialog. Fewer than 15 samples shows the WPF error. Disable the shortcut or clash G with panic/pause and confirm refusal.
2. WPF `ConditioningControlPanel/MainWindow/MainWindow.SessionIO.cs`, `ConditioningControlPanel/MainWindow/MainWindow.BlinkTrainer.cs` — Rows: `win-webcam-quick-recal`
   On a native Wayland desktop supporting GlobalShortcuts, accept the compositor requests for Quick Recalibrate and Camera start/stop. With a native Wayland application focused, invoke both shortcuts and repeat step 1. Reassign chords in the compositor consent dialog and confirm the quick-recal hint names the assigned triggers. Decline a request and confirm no armed hint; the setup buttons and camera in-window shortcut remain usable. Close the shell or change/disable a binding while consent is pending; accepting late must not resurrect it. Disable Camera Shortcut Global and confirm its global binding is released. With stale camera consent, invoke the camera chord and confirm the restored shell asks for consent before starting, with start/stop toasts. A compositor without GlobalShortcuts leaves the global Wayland chords unavailable.
