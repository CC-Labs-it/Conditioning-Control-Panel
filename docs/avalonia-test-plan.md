1. Open Companion → Workshop → Behavior, set chat to Ctrl+Shift+J with global enabled, focus another application, and press the chord. The shell restores, the tube appears, and chat opens. Disable global and confirm the chord works only inside the app. Rebind via Settings → Devices too. WPF `MainWindow/MainWindow.SessionIO.cs:1120-1241`. Rows: `views-companion-workshop-behavior-cell`.
2. Set the camera global chord, focus another application, and toggle it twice. Starting requires current camera consent; stopping shows the stopped toast. Reserve a chord in another application and confirm rejection preserves in-window fallback and the other slot. Close the app and confirm its chords are released. WPF `Services/Input/GlobalHotkeyService.cs`, `MainWindow/MainWindow.SessionIO.cs:1320-1443`. Rows: `views-companion-workshop-behavior-cell`.
3. Change idle interval to 20 seconds. Confirm a full new interval elapses before idle chatter; speech and listening restart it. Mute master volume or keep the companion speaking: idle must not interrupt. Eligible idle lines rotate before repeating, with the last line excluded at recycle. Restart the app and confirm persisted rotation. WPF `AvatarTube/AvatarTubeWindow.Speech.cs:1534-1591`, `Services/Companion/BarkService.cs:1178-1226`. Rows: `views-companion-workshop-behavior-cell`.
4. Change bubble duration, mute whispers and mute voice lines; confirm saved polarity and live tube/browser quick menu mirroring. Reveal Behavior again and confirm midnight ownership is reread without writing settings. WPF `Views/Controls/Companion/Runtime/WorkshopBehaviorCell.xaml.cs`, `MainWindow/MainWindow.Patreon.cs`. Rows: `views-companion-workshop-behavior-cell`.
